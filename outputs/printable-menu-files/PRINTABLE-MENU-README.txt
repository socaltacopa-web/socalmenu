SoCal Tacos Printable Menu

Open this file:
socal-tacos-printable-menu.html

Then click the big "Print / Save PDF" button at the top.

To make a PDF:
1. Open socal-tacos-printable-menu.html.
2. Click Print / Save PDF.
3. Choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
4. Click Save.

The menu has multiple pages and includes the food pictures used by the ordering website.
